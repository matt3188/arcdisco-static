Parties

We provide mobile disco services for all occasions including but not limited too birthday parties, anniversaries, retirement and leaving parties, dedicated decade nights, etc.
At all functions we will supply a sound system appropriate to your needs and run it at volumes that meet your requirements. Because we use intelligent lighting, which is totally controllable, you can rest assured that if you do not want wildly flashing light, you do not have to have them.

Enquire here for a quote and our availability for your event.

 

• All of our bookings are confirmed in writing and we issue contracts to all our clients
• Our equipment is modern, well maintained and tested for electrical safety (P.A.T)
• Lists of your favourite tunes, to be played at your party, are welcome
• Requests will be accepted on the night (with your permission)
• In addition we aim to reply to all emails within a 24 hour period and always return your phone calls

We carry a wide variety of music from the 1950’s to the present day. If you have any special musical requests these should be made clear at the time of booking to avoid disappointment.
If you need a disco that will play the kind of music you want to hear then call us.

Book A.R.C. Disco and we won’t let you down, we do not hire other Dj’s to do events we are booked for.
